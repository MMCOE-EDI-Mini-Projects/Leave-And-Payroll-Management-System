Connecting Foundation Courses to the Project Theme
Foundation courses from previous semesters provide the theoretical knowledge required for the mini project.
The project theme integrates concepts from multiple subjects to solve a practical problem.
Students apply knowledge from core courses such as programming, databases, networking, electronics, mathematics, or domain-specific subjects (as applicable).
The project demonstrates how individual course concepts work together in a real-world application.
This integration strengthens conceptual understanding and develops practical, interdisciplinary skills
